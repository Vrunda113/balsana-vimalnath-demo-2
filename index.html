• Legends in Stone

A Story Written in Earth

“For thousands of years, the land of Balsana guarded a secret known only to the heavens.”

The historical legend of Balsana Vimalnath Tirth dates back centuries. The divine Pratimaji was safely embedded within the deep earth, waiting for the spiritual alignment of humanity. Local folklore speaks of spiritual vibrations felt by the villagers of Balsana, ultimately leading to the reverent excavation of the beautiful idol.

Today, pilgrims from across the globe gather at this exact coordinate where the material world opened its hands to release the divine embodiment of purity — Shri Vimalnath Bhagwan.
